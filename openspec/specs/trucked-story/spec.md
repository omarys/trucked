### Requirement: GuiltPolicy

The system SHALL the system shall choose guilt's action intent as a force strike, activating gambit only when recklessness is below 25.

#### Scenario: GuiltLowRecklessnessGambit
- **GIVEN** Guilt combatant has Recklessness value below 25
- **WHEN** guilt attack policy is evaluated
- **THEN** A StandardAttack with ForceStrike and gambit enabled is returned

#### Scenario: GuiltStandardStrike
- **GIVEN** Guilt combatant has Recklessness value of 25 or greater
- **WHEN** guilt attack policy is evaluated
- **THEN** A StandardAttack with ForceStrike without gambit is returned

### Requirement: DenialPolicy

The system SHALL the system shall choose denial's action intent as finesse gambit when clone count is under 2 and recklessness is under 70; otherwise it shall choose synaptic glamour.

#### Scenario: DenialPhasesOutWithClonesLow
- **GIVEN** Denial has fewer than 2 MirrorClones and Recklessness below 70
- **WHEN** denial attack policy is evaluated
- **THEN** A StandardAttack with TraumaAttack FinesseGambit is returned

#### Scenario: DenialAttacksWithGlamourWhenClonesActiveOrReckless
- **GIVEN** Denial has 2 or more MirrorClones or Recklessness at 70 or higher
- **WHEN** denial attack policy is evaluated
- **THEN** A StandardAttack with SynapticGlamour evaluated with gambit criteria is returned

### Requirement: AngerPolicy

The system SHALL the system shall choose anger's action intent as force gambit when recklessness is below 65, and a wild force strike gambit when recklessness is 65 or higher.

#### Scenario: AngerEruptsWhileComposed
- **GIVEN** Anger has Recklessness below 65
- **WHEN** anger attack policy is evaluated
- **THEN** A StandardAttack with TraumaAttack ForceGambit is returned

#### Scenario: AngerSwingsWildWhenReckless
- **GIVEN** Anger has Recklessness at 65 or higher
- **WHEN** anger attack policy is evaluated
- **THEN** A StandardAttack with ForceStrike and gambit forced true is returned

### Requirement: BargainingPolicy

The system SHALL the system shall choose bargaining's action intent as acumen gambit when opponent morale is above 40 and recklessness is below 65; otherwise it shall choose acumen interrogation.

#### Scenario: BargainingTradesOnOpponentMorale
- **GIVEN** Opponent has Morale above 40 and Bargaining has Recklessness below 65
- **WHEN** bargaining attack policy is evaluated
- **THEN** A StandardAttack with TraumaAttack AcumenGambit is returned

#### Scenario: BargainingInterrogatesDepletedOpponentOrHighRecklessness
- **GIVEN** Opponent Morale is 40 or lower, or Bargaining Recklessness is 65 or higher
- **WHEN** bargaining attack policy is evaluated
- **THEN** A StandardAttack with AcumenInterrogation evaluated with gambit criteria is returned

### Requirement: ContinueToNextEvent

The system SHALL the runner shall advance the story flow and emit narrative events until reaching an active choice point, a combat encounter trigger, or story completion.

#### Scenario: AdvanceStoryText
- **GIVEN** The story has continuing narrative text and no pending combat encounter
- **WHEN** ContinueToNextEvent is called
- **THEN** It SHALL return a list containing TextProduced events for each non-empty line of text

#### Scenario: TriggerCombatEncounter
- **GIVEN** The Ink story invokes the external start_combat function with an enemy identifier
- **WHEN** ContinueToNextEvent evaluates the story flow
- **THEN** It SHALL stop text continuation, instantiate the enemy combatant, and return a CombatInitiated event

### Requirement: ResolveCombat

The system SHALL the runner shall resolve the outcome of an active combat encounter, update story state, and route the narrative path accordingly.

#### Scenario: PlayerVictory
- **GIVEN** A combat encounter concludes with PlayerVictorious
- **WHEN** ResolveCombat is called with a target knot or default
- **THEN** The story path SHALL be routed to the specified nextKnot or 'post_combat'

#### Scenario: PlayerDefeat
- **GIVEN** A combat encounter concludes with PlayerDefeated
- **WHEN** ResolveCombat is called
- **THEN** IsGameOver SHALL be set to true, the death animation callback executed, and the story path routed to
'game_over_respawn'
